Walk into every meeting already caught up

Meeting Prep remembers every conversation you have with a contact. Before your next meeting, it tells you what was discussed, what you promised, what they promised, and what to watch for.

The problem

Before a call, most people quickly scan old notes and hope they remember the important parts. Promises get missed, a late delivery is forgotten, and the other person notices. A tool that only answers questions has nothing to work with. This one remembers between meetings, so it gets better the more you use it.

Screen recording → Instant briefing

After any meeting, just upload the screen recording (Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Loom, etc.) along with optional notes. Meeting Prep will:

  • Transcribe the recording
  • Extract what was discussed, what each side promised, and any concerns
  • Store everything as lasting memories in Hindsight
  • Generate a clear prep briefing for your next conversation with that contact

You no longer need to write perfect notes. The recording is enough.

Where Hindsight memory is used

One memory bank per contact
Everything about one person stays completely separate from everyone else, so a briefing never mixes up different relationships.
Retain
Every promise, concern and topic is stored as a short, dated statement instead of one long transcript. That makes it easier to find later.
Recall and reflect
Before a meeting, the agent pulls the relevant memories and summarises the relationship. The briefing is built from that, and the memories are shown on the page so you can check them.
A separate style memory
Feedback like “keep it under 150 words” is stored in its own bank and applies to every contact’s briefing from then on.

What it doesn’t do yet

  • It doesn’t automatically connect to your calendar.
  • It’s built for one user on one machine (no accounts or sharing).
  • Briefings are only as good as the recording and notes you give it.
